# Paragon PR7-S: notes for Claude

**Paragon PR7-S** is the Paragon PR7 family's **studio**: a standalone DAW (AGPLv3, JUCE, a program, not a plugin) with a workflow **inspired by Cubase** and the PR7 family's look. The owner chose the name (S for studio).

| Member | Repo |
|---|---|
| PR7-G (guitar) | `ARYEZ/Paragon-X1` (now Paragon-PR7-G) |
| PR7-B (bass) | `ARYEZ/Paragon-PR7-B` |
| PR7-V (vocals) | `ARYEZ/Paragon-PR7-V` |
| PR7-C (channel strip) | `ARYEZ/Paragon-PR7-C` |

## The plan (agreed with the owner; one release each)

1. **0.1.0, done: MIDI and instruments** (the owner's choice to start). Instrument tracks (the built-in Paragon Synth or VST3 instruments), MIDI recording from a keyboard or the on-screen one, the arrangement, transport, click, cycle, songs saved as `.pr7s`, undo.
2. **0.2.0, done: the piano roll (key editor):** draw, move, copy, resize, delete notes; velocity lane; quantize; in the lower zone, following the selected part.
3. **0.3.0, done: the mixer:** channel strips, inserts (VST3 effects, including our PR7 plugins; 16 slots since 0.5.1), master bus with its own inserts; the on-screen keyboard removed (the owner asked: the lower zone needs the room).
4. **0.4.0, done: audio tracks:** record through the interface (mono or stereo input, latency-compensated), clips with waveform, trim start/end, fades, monitoring through inserts; ASIO on Windows.
5. **0.5.0, done: export** (a mixdown to WAV; the owner agreed to split automation off). **0.5.1:** fixes from checking 0.1–0.5 against the Cubase 15 manual, and family colours only.
6. **0.6.0, done: automation** (Touch writing; lanes, R/W, drawing and editing; the master's row).
7. **0.7.0, done: sends, FX and group channels** (the owner's choice after automation). **0.7.1:** MIDI file import (the owner asked). **0.7.2:** zoom (H in, G out, Shift+F), Auto-Scroll (F), scroll bars, the lower zone resizable, the mixer's rack hidden (the owner asked; the owner remembered G/H the other way round, the manual has H in).
8. Later: editing tools (split, glue, tool keys; suggested as 0.8.0), takes and comping, MIDI controller lanes, drum editor, markers... as the owner asks.

Be honest about scale: Cubase took a large team decades. Say where it stands.

## Colours (the owner's rule, 0.5.1)

Only the family's colours: anything along the **red / pink / orange** gradient, **pearl white** and **pearl onyx** (the dark panels and greys). No blue, green, violet, aqua or yellow anywhere (track colours included: `trackColour`, tested). PR7-G's signal-chain colours were removed from `ParagonColours`. The shared `LedDisplay` still has the plugins' green "in tune" for a tuner, which PR7-S never shows.

## The Cubase manual (0.5.1)

The owner wants Cubase **15**'s behaviour. Its manual is the **Cubase Pro 15 Help** on www.steinberg.help (allowed in this environment's network; archive.steinberg.help isn't). It's a Fluid Topics site with a JSON API:
- `curl https://www.steinberg.help/api/khub/maps` lists the manuals (Cubase Pro Help 15.0.30, English: map id `NlutMEjaYjAqk1bhdcAc5g`).
- `.../api/khub/maps/<map id>/toc` is the table of contents (nodes with `title`, `contentId`, `children`).
- `.../api/khub/maps/<map id>/topics/<contentId>/content` is a page's HTML.

Read the chapter before building a feature "as Cubase does", and quote it. 0.5.1 checked 0.1–0.5 against it. Still different, and left so on purpose:
- **Keys (the owner's choice: they stay):** R records (Cubase: R arms, Num * records), / cycles (Cubase: Num /), Home goes to the start (Cubase: Num . or ,), Escape closes the key editor (Cubase: Return opens/closes the editor).
- Only one track armed at a time; the export's "include the click" (our addition); the "<song> Audio" folder.

Cubase defaults it confirmed: Space, C, Delete, Ctrl+D, Ctrl+Z/Shift+Z, Ctrl+N/O/S/Shift+S, Ctrl+E, F3 (MixConsole), Alt+F3 (in the lower zone), Q, the key editor's tool keys 1/5/8, 120 ticks per sixteenth, middle C = C3, +6 dB fader top, the balance pan.

## Code layout

- `source/engine/`: no screen needed, fully testable.
  - `Project`: the song as plain data (tempo, signature, cycle, click, tracks, parts, notes in **beats** = quarter notes, each track's `inserts`, the master's volume and `masterInserts`), XML save/open, bar/beat formatting.
  - `Engine`: the audio device callback. Plays each track's events through its instrument (`AudioPluginInstance`), the cycle, the click, mix (volume, balance pan, mute, solo), live MIDI (devices via `MidiMessageCollector`, the on-screen `MidiKeyboardState`) into the live track, and records note on/off with their beats into a lock-free FIFO. **Threads:** the audio thread only `ScopedTryLock`s (a missed lock = one silent block); the message thread prepares everything outside the lock and swaps under it (`update`, `setInstrument`, `removeTrack`). No allocation on the audio thread. Signal path: instrument, its inserts in order, fader and pan, into the master buffer; master inserts, master fader, output; the click after the master. Effects are prepared stereo if they allow it, else as they are (mono in: L+R mixed; mono out: to both). Inserts by `insertEffect` / `removeEffect` (bus id 0 = master): 16 slots in fixed places (an `Insert` can be empty: `isEmpty()`; none after the last used), the first `preFaderSlots` before the fader and pan, the rest after (per track, and `masterPreFaderSlots`); the fader is applied to the track's own buffer, then the post-fader inserts, then the sum. Do sample work on raw pointers (FloatVectorOperations), not `AudioBuffer` methods: a buffer remembers being cleared and its own methods then skip work, even after something wrote to it through another view (it silenced mono effects once). Meters have two readers (0: transport and track list, 1: the mixer) so they don't take each other's peaks.
  - **Audio tracks:** `Track::Kind::audio` (input, stereoInput, monitor); their parts are clips (`Part::audioFile`, `audioOffset` seconds, `fadeIn`/`fadeOut` seconds; start/length in beats). `Engine::addAudioTrack`, clips played sample-accurately per segment of the block (`playClips`), input monitoring through the track's inserts, recording through a `ThreadedWriter` set with `setRecorder` (written on the audio thread, the first sample's song position in `getAudioRecordStart`; in a cycle every pass goes to the file and the clip plays the last complete one, as Cubase's Replace mode). `Studio`: recordings in `getAudioFolder()` ("<song> Audio" beside the song, else Documents/Paragon PR7-S/Unsaved Recordings/<time>; save copies them over), loaded whole and resampled to the engine's rate (`getAudio`, with peaks for drawing), latency compensation from the device (`setLatencyForTests`). `Engine::render` takes an input buffer for tests. Moving the position while stopped sets `rebase` (the clock restarts there even if play comes before a block).
  - **Export:** `Studio::beginExport` / `renderExport` / `endExport` (`exportMix` does all three). Begin takes the engine off the interface, re-prepares it at the export's rate, plays a copy of the song (no cycle, the click as asked, parts cut at the end so the ring-out holds only what's sounding; the ring-out goes on until 0.25 s below -100 dB, up to `tailSeconds`: 30 for the whole song, 0 for the cycle; 16, 24 or 32-bit float) and sets every plugin non-realtime (`Engine::setNonRealtime`); render runs on any thread (MainView's `ThreadWithProgressWindow`); end puts everything back and deletes an unfinished file. `Windows::ExportPage` asks what (tests drive it directly; it's shown as a modal `DialogWindow`). `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_EXPORT=1` draws it over the snapshot.
  - **Automation (0.6.0):** `AutomationLane` (points: beat and value 0–1, the control's travel; volume's is the fader's law `volumeToTravel`; mute steps, the rest ramp), on tracks and the master; R/W per track (`automationRead`/`Write`; W turns R on), Mute Automation per lane. The engine plays lanes with R on: plugin knobs once a block before the plugin, volume/pan/mute every 32 samples ramped, at the playhead when stopped; `getMixNow` for the faders. Studio: show/hide, append, `setLaneControl` (non-destructive: the old control's points kept hidden), remove, mute, points (turns R on), R/W all. Lane edits are undo steps; undo leaves what's shown and R/W. Removing or replacing an insert takes its lanes (from the undo history too). The manual's chapter: `Automation` in the TOC. **Writing:** `grabControl` / `releaseControl` (the UI calls them on a fader's or knob's drag start and end; mute's click does both), values through `setVolume`/`setPan`/`setMute`/`setMasterVolume`/`setKnob`; a `WritePass` per control; `Engine::setHeld` keeps a held control's lane out of playback; punch out on release (Touch: back to what was there over `returnSeconds`), stop, W off, a jump, the cycle coming round (`Engine::getCycleCount`); mute latches until stop. Plugin knobs: a `KnobWatcher` (AudioProcessorListener) per plugin, removed before the plugin is deleted (`unwatch` in effectGoing, instrument changes, track removal, new/open). **On screen:** `Timeline::layout` (rows: track, its shown lanes, MASTER and its lanes; laneHeight 48, masterHeight 40, our choice) for both views; `AutomationKeys` (R pink, W red, writing orange: only on the plain white finish; neon keys light pale gold as the other keys do); TrackList `LaneRow` / `MasterRow` / `Arrow`; ArrangeView paints lanes and edits points (`Drag::points/band/draw`, `PointSelection`, `commitPoints`: one undo step); MainView's header R/W, Alt+R/W, Delete for points, Esc to cancel. The master's row is our design (the manual doesn't describe an output channel track).
  - **Sends, FX and group channels (0.7.0):** `Track::Kind::fx` / `group` (`isChannel()`: no parts, no instrument, never armed). `Track::output` (0 master, or a group's id; FX and group channels always to the master in 0.7.0, so routing can't loop), `Track::sends` (up to `Project::maxSends` 8, by slot: `Send` target FX id, levelDb, on, preFader) and `sendsBypass`. Engine: `addChannel`; roles source/group/fx processed in that order; each channel's `bus` collects what's routed or sent to it; pre-fader sends after the pre-fader inserts, post-fader after the fader, pan and post inserts (`runSends`, gain ramped per block); solo of a track never silences channels. Automation `Target::send` (slot = send). Studio: `addFxChannel` ("FX n-<effect>"), `addGroupChannel`, `setOutput`, `setSend`, `setSendLevel` (writes automation like a fader), `removeSend`, `setSendsBypass`; removing a channel resets what pointed at it; `Project::fromXml` resets routing that leads nowhere. UI: add-track menu, channel rows, track menu Output; MixerView INSERTS/SENDS rack toggle (our design), `SendSlot`, OUT button. Manual pages: Send Effects, Effect Channel Tracks, Group Channel Tracks (TOC). Defaults that are our choice: 8 sends, new send post-fader at 0 dB.
  - **MIDI file import (0.7.1):** `MidiImport.h/.cpp`: `readMidiFile` (JUCE `MidiFile`; type 0 split by channel; ticks per quarter or SMPTE; the first tempo and time signature; tracks without notes left out). `Studio::importMidi` (new song: the file's tempo and signature, the empty default track removed, no undo before it; else into this song at a beat, its tempo kept): a Paragon Synth track per imported track, one part over whole bars. MainView: IMPORT button (asks new song or this one when the song has parts) and `FileDragAndDropTarget` (a .mid at the bar dropped on). Manual pages: Importing Audio and MIDI Files > MIDI File Import. Defaults that are our choice: see the 0.7.1 notes.
  - `ParagonSynth`: the built-in instrument (an `AudioPluginInstance`, so it's played like a VST3).
  - `PluginHost`: VST3 format manager and the known-plugin list (kept in the settings file); `getInstruments` / `getEffects`, `create` / `createEffect`.
  - `Project` also has `quantize` (note starts to the *song's* grid; same-pitch twins merge) and `noteName` (middle C = C3, as in Cubase).
  - `Studio`: message-thread controller; owns project, engine, host, `AudioDeviceManager`, settings; every action; turns recordings into parts; gives every part an `id` (not saved; `findPart`) so the key editor keeps its part when indices change; `setNotes` replaces a part's notes (one undo step per editor gesture); the mixer's actions (`addInsert`, `replaceInsert`, `removeInsert`, `setInsertBypass`, `setMasterVolume`; `onEffectGoing` closes an effect's window before it's deleted; `setVolume`/`setPan` with `undoable = false` while a fader is dragged); adding a track records an undo state (so the next change to it can be undone); undo (parts, song settings, track names and mix; not adding/removing tracks); `onInstrumentGoing` lets the UI close an instrument's window **before** the instance is deleted (a plugin editor must never outlive its processor).
- `source/ui/`: `MainView` (header, `TransportBar`, `TrackList` | `ArrangeView`, and the lower zone below them: `KeyEditor` or `MixerView`, one docked at a time, either in its own window instead; MainView's `Zone`s handle both; keys go to the editor after a click in it; no on-screen keyboard since 0.3.0), `Windows` (instrument editor, settings, plugins), `Pearl.h` (the pearl unit surface). From PR7-C: `ParagonLookAndFeel`, `Finish` (settings file "Paragon PR7-S"), `LedDisplay`, `Logo` (the mark with "-S"), `LevelMeter`.
  - `MixerView`: a `Strip` per track and the master's (16 insert slots, pan, M/S/R/W, fader following automation while R is on, with the PR7 fader look lit from the bottom, meter). Insert menus are async popups (not testable directly; the studio actions are).
  - `LevelMeter`: PR7-C's meter (segments every 3 px, `ParagonColours::meterColour` orange to pink-red, held peak 1.5 s), dBFS -60 to +6. `paintSegments` and `Ballistics` are shared by the transport's OUT, the track list (horizontal) and the mixer.
  - `Windows::ZoneWindow`: the key editor or the mixer in its own window (WINDOW / DOCK). F3 toggles the mixer's window, Alt+F3 and MIXER the docked mixer (`toggleMixerWindow` / `toggleMixerDocked`), as Cubase's F3 / Alt+F3 (confirmed with the manual, 0.5.1). Shows the editor without owning it; MainView moves it in and out (`setKeyEditorInWindow`), remembers the choice and the window's state in the settings file. The window is never deleted inside its own click (callAsync).
  - Tests with a real window: don't expect sizes bigger than the screen (macOS keeps a window within the screen's height; CI's screens are small). 0.2.1 failed on macOS CI for that and was never released.
  - Tests on Linux register the window manager's atoms first (`tests/TestMain.cpp`): with no window manager on the virtual display, JUCE fails creating a real window.
  - **Getting around (0.7.2):** `Timeline::autoScroll` / `scrollLength` (8 bars past the last part, at least 32: our choice) / `zoomStep` (1.5, our choice); `ArrangeView::zoom` / `zoomFull` / `followPlayhead` (Page Scroll: playing, or a jump while stopped); the key editor has its own (`KeyEditor::zoom`, `setAutoScroll`, its scroll bars inside it: `scrollBarSize`). MainView: G/H/F/Shift+F routed as the other keys (to the editor after a click in it), the arrangement's scroll bars (`refreshScrollBars`, `scrollBarMoved`), `ZoneDivider` and `setDockedHeight` (each zone's `dockedHeight`, settings `keyEditorHeight` / `mixerHeight`, between `minimumHeight` and `minimumArrangeHeight`); TrackList's AUTO-SCROLL key; settings `arrangeAutoScroll`, `keyEditorAutoScroll`, `mixerRack`. MixerView `setRackShown` (the lit INSERTS/SENDS clicked again), `getMinimumHeight`, `onMinimumHeightChange`. `ParagonLookAndFeel::drawScrollbar` (JUCE's own is blue). Manual pages: Zooming in the Project Window, Zoom Submenu, Auto-Scroll Settings Menu. Not done yet: vertical zoom, Stationary Cursor, Suspend Auto-Scroll When Editing.
  - `KeyEditor`: the piano roll. Works on its own copy of the notes during a drag and calls `Studio::setNotes` once on release. Its own clipboard (Ctrl+C/X/V; paste at the playhead if in the part, else the beat after the copied notes, or another part's start); MainView sends Ctrl+C/X/V and Shift+A to it whenever it's open. Tests drive it with real `MouseEvent`s (`tests/UiTests.cpp`).
  - **Fonts:** Bebas Neue everywhere (the owner's choice, 0.3.1) through `ParagonLookAndFeel::textFont` / `titleFont` (every view's `font()` helper calls them); Bebas is drawn `bebasScale` (1.3x) larger to match Inter's size. `DisplayFont` also has `inter` and `bebasTitles` for comparing renders (`PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_FONT=inter|titles`). Not the LED screens (Inter, to match their digits) or tooltips. The other PR7s still use Inter.
- `PR7SCore` (CMake) is a static library with everything but `Main.cpp`, so the app and the tests run the same code.

## Build, test, check

- **Build:** `cmake -B build && cmake --build build -j8`. JUCE 9.0.4 and Catch2 are fetched by CMake (locally you can point `FETCHCONTENT_SOURCE_DIR_JUCE` at another checkout).
- **Tests:** `xvfb-run -a build/tests/ParagonPR7STests`. All must pass. Engine tests use `Probe` (tests/TestHelpers.h), an instrument that logs every MIDI event with its sample: check timing to the sample, not just "it played". Audio tests (`tests/AudioTests.cpp`) use `ramp` recordings (every sample told apart) and render with an input buffer. Mixer tests (`tests/MixerTests.cpp`) use `SteadyTone` (a DC level per side) and `TestEffect` (`timesEffect`, `plusEffect`, mono ones) and read levels off the output.
- **Render:** `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT=studio.png xvfb-run -a build/tests/ParagonPR7STests "[snapshot]"` (a demo song, with the key editor open on the lead part). `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_WIDTH=3000` for 2x, `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_FINISH=black|neon`, `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_ZONE=mixer` for the mixer (=none for no lower zone; the demo has a guitar audio track), `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_FONT=inter|titles` for the other font choices (stand-in effects named after the family's plugins). `"[snapshot-window]"` renders the key editor's own window (`PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_SIZE`, default 5760x1000: three screens; with `PARAGON_SNAPSHOT_ZONE=mixer`, the mixer's window with an eight-track song, default 1920x1000). Look, crop and zoom before claiming a visual change is right.
- No pluginval: it's an app. Windows is built with the static C++ runtime.

## Releases

- Bump `project(ParagonPR7S VERSION x.y.z)` and add a `## x.y.z` CHANGELOG section, then push. CI builds Windows, macOS and Linux, runs the tests, and publishes a release with `ParagonPR7-S-vX.Y.Z-<OS>.zip` (the app itself).
- About 15 minutes after pushing, check the release has all three zips **and its tag is your commit**.
- The owner installs on **Windows** and works in **Cubase** and BeatMaker.

## Open questions (ask, don't guess)

- **ASIO on Windows (settled, 0.4.0):** the ASIO SDK is dual-licensed, proprietary or GPLv3 (since October 2025); PR7-S uses the GPLv3 option. It isn't on GitHub: Windows CI downloads it from steinberg.net/asiosdk, checks a GPL licence text is in it, and passes `PARAGON_ASIO_SDK` (CMake turns on `JUCE_ASIO`). If that fails, the build goes on without ASIO and warns: check the Windows job's log for "ASIO SDK (GPLv3) at" after each release. steinberg.net isn't reachable from the cloud sessions, so this can only be checked in CI.
- A **Cubase Operation Manual** from the owner helps for behaviour and shortcuts (never for names or graphics).
