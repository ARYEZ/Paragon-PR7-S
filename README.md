# Paragon PR7-S

**Paragon PR7-S** is the Paragon PR7 family's studio: a standalone DAW for recording and producing music, with a workflow inspired by Cubase and the look of the PR7 family. It's built with [JUCE](https://juce.com).

The other members of the family are PR7-G (guitar), PR7-B (bass), PR7-V (vocals) and PR7-C (channel strip).

It's built in stages, a release at a time. The first stage was MIDI recording with VST3 instruments; the second, the piano roll (key editor); the third, the mixer with VST3 effects. Later stages add audio tracks, and then automation and export.
