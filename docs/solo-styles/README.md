# Solo styles: research for the solo engine

The owner asked for solos written in the manner of players they love: their techniques and habits, never their actual solos. Each sheet here describes a player's style in terms a rule-based MIDI generator can use (notes, density, phrasing, rhythm, bends, vibrato, techniques, a recipe), marks what a source supports [S] and what is inference [I], and lists its sources. No solo or melody of any player is written out.

The owner's order:

| Round | Players |
|---|---|
| 1 | David Gilmour, Joe Perry, Eddie Van Halen, Jimmy Page |
| 2 | Dimebag Darrell, Kirk Hammett, Zakk Wylde, John Petrucci |
| 3 | C.C. DeVille, Mick Mars, Slash, Prince |
| 4 | Synyster Gates, Clint Lowery, Jerry Cantrell, Kurt Cobain |

**The owner's decisions:**
- **Names:** the app uses our own descriptive names for the styles, never the players' names (to be safe); the sheets here keep the players' names as research notes.
- **Dive bombs:** wanted. They need a big pitch bend range: the owner sets Shreddage's Pitch Bend Range and the INSTRUMENTS profile's BEND RANGE to the same number (12 or more); bends are written by that range, so ordinary whole-step bends stay right.
- **The owner's Shreddage keys:** Sustain C-1, Power Chords C#-1 (theirs); the rest as the Jupiter manual lists them: Staccato D-1, Tremolo F#-1, Choke G-1, Harmonics G#-1, Tapping A-1, FX A#-1; legato (hammer-on, pull-off, slide) by overlapping notes.

Round 1: [Gilmour](gilmour.md), [Joe Perry](joe-perry.md), [Eddie Van Halen](eddie-van-halen.md), [Jimmy Page](jimmy-page.md).

## Round 1 side by side (for the engine's settings; revised from the full articles)

| | Gilmour | Joe Perry | Van Halen | Page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Notes | minor pent. + 2 and 6 (Dorian), b5 passing; major pent. + 4 on major chords; phrases start/end on roots and 5ths | minor pent. + b5 + major 6th; minor and major 3rd; Mixolydian | blues + b5/5 zigzags; "Van Halen scale" for fast runs; tapped triads, tap at the 12th | minor pent. 12th-fret box + b5, 9, natural 6; major/minor mixing a core habit; b3 a quarter tone sharp |
| Density | 2-6 notes a bar, 6-12 at the peak [I] | 4-8, bursts 12-16 [I] | melody 1-2 a beat, bursts 6-8 a beat (32nds) | melody 4-8, "crammed" bursts 12-24 |
| Space | rests core; the fastest burst near the end, short | space "to the max" | 20-30% in melody [I] | 1-2 beats [I] |
| Phrases | a motif that keeps coming back; sung leaps | call and response; rarely the same twice | cells across descending string pairs; up a 2nd / down a 3rd endings | thematic repetition; 1.5-beat or six-16th licks rotating against the bar |
| Rhythm | quarters, 8ths, triplets; 3 against 4 | swung 16ths (2:1) when the song swings; behind the beat | swung, push-pull; groupings of 3, 5, 7 | triplets, sextuplets, 32nds; way behind the beat at points |
| Bends | whole steps most; 3-4 semitones; two-stage, multiple, pre-bend; unison | constant; whole, half, 1.5; double-stop, unison | up to a major third; pairs of bends; bends with taps | half to 2.5 steps; unison, pedal-steel, smear openings |
| Vibrato | a quarter-step dip on bends, not fast; restrained | wide, varied speed | wide; bar vibrato | fast, steady, swinging below the bent note |
| Signature | space, held notes, building arc, returning motif | swing, slides in, 6th double stops, loose | tapping, legato, harmonics (tapped 12/7/5/4 frets up), whammy: scoops, dips, dives, whinny | repeated licks, displacement, a repeated bend into a pull-off climax |

Numbers marked [I] in the sheets are starting points to tune by ear. Round 1 was first researched from search summaries, then revised from the full articles (the environment now allows the lesson sites).

## Round 2 side by side

| | Dimebag | Hammett | Wylde | Petrucci |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Notes | minor pent. / blues + major 3rd, major 6th, b5; chromatic moves anywhere ("no rules") | minor pent. home, b5 targets, "bluesier" now; harmonic minor / Phrygian colour sparingly | minor pent. + b5 by design (he crossed out exotic scales); major pent. on Southern/ballad parts; rare chromatic passing notes | diatonic modes (natural minor, Mixolydian), harmonic minor, diminished; seventh-chord arpeggios, stacked triads; a bluesy side for contrast |
| Density | melodic start, bursts of 16ths/sextuplets [I numbers] | 4-8 bar melodic opening, then repeated-lick bursts [I numbers] | 3-8 a bar melodic, 16-24 in bursts [I] | 3-6 a bar singing, 16ths/sextuplets/16th triplets in bursts |
| Phrases | a motif repeated an octave up/down; a lick moved a fret at a time; symmetrical runs | a short lick repeated 4-8 times; a three-beat lick drifting across the bar; the opening idea back at the end | melody, then "the afterburners near the end"; beginning, middle, end | composed to the song's story, "big curves" to a climax, ideas from the song's melody, never repeating himself |
| Rhythm | eight-note cells in a 16th-triplet feel, shifting against the beat | picked triplets/sextuplets; enters on an upbeat; plays through the bar line | six-note descending groups as 16ths (drifting) or sextuplets (on the beat) | six-note cells two steps apart; groups of 5 and 7; phrases ending off the one |
| Bends | "Texas" half step from below, slow; wide multi-finger bends; unison, double stops | expressive, bend and release, a semitone on a double stop [sizes I] | varied speeds ("shades of the bend"); bend and tap | slow, perfectly in tune (the melodic gear) |
| Vibrato | aggressive; whammy vibrato on harmonics | wide, consistent | very wide (a tone; a minor 3rd on pinches), at full width at once, on every phrase end | a repeated bend up and back |
| Signature | harmonic screams (bar dumped, pulled up), dive bombs, pinch harmonics at the end | pentatonic speed, repeated licks, trills, wah (out of scope), ends "dripping up high" | pinch-harmonic squeals in every gap, alternate-picked pentatonic boxes linked by slides, no whammy | alternate-picked runs, sweeps, legato, odd groupings, something "outside" at the climax |

Names in the app (ours, the owner approved): SCREAM & DIVE (Dimebag), THRASH FIRE (Hammett), PINCH SQUEAL (Wylde), PROG PRECISION (Petrucci); 0.67.0.

## Round 3 side by side

| | DeVille | Mars | Slash | Prince |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Notes | blues-rooted hard rock [I scales]; some "outside" colour around 1990 | a bluesman, not scale-based; [I] minor pent. + blues | minor pent. + b5; b6 and harmonic minor in dramatic solos; major pent. over happy major chords (no b3) | major pent. + blue b3, b7 (writers); "crazy pentatonic stuff" |
| Density | sustained notes, the speed saved for "the last possible moment" | a melody "you can hum", not "a barrage of notes"; 3-6 a bar [I] | "I can sit on one note forever"; fast is "an energy thing" | "hold a high note... while you play 100 notes"; bursts |
| Phrases | long bends answered by melodic bursts; lyrical double stops | weaving round the singer, a two-bar phrase and its answer | melodic first half, wilder second; a four-note sequence up a step; a motif an octave up | a home motif brought back higher; escalating bends to a scream |
| Rhythm | against the beat at times | an occasional fast single-string burst | loose, behind the beat; three against four; 32nd/triplet flurries between melody notes | rhythm first; funk: very short notes; triplets natural |
| Bends | long drawn-out, slow in ballads, pre-bends | held, "get everything out of a note" | slow, a little loose, held and released several times; unison, oblique | subtle at first, ever bigger |
| Vibrato | lots [weak source] | "aggressive" [weak source]; whammy as a voice | expressive | "Santana-level" |
| Signature | a fast picked burst at the very end | whammy dips, slide (one album) | melody first, behind the beat, no tapping | held screaming high notes, one outburst outside the key at the peak |
