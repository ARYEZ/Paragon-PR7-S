# Jimmy Page: lead style (technique sheet)

Researched for the studio's solo engine. Techniques and habits only; no solo or melody of his is written out here.
**[S]** = a source supports it, **[I]** = inference (the researcher could read only search excerpts; Guitar Techniques' full style analysis is paywalled; no source gave numbers).

## 1. Note choice
- Minor pentatonic, mostly the box round the 12th fret, working the top three strings [S]; the blues b5 often [S]; the 2nd/9th often [S] ([I] about 10% of non-chord-tone choices).
- The major 6th (Dorian) in modal pieces ("No Quarter", "Over the Hills" described as Dorian) [S]; Aeolian and Dorian mixed [S]. [I] In a minor key the natural 6 over the b6 about 60% of the time.
- The minor 3rd often bent a quarter tone sharp [S]. [I] +0.25-0.5 semitone on about half the b3s; on major chords the b3 bent toward the major 3rd.
- Phrases resolve onto a target note [S]. [I] Phrase ends on the root, 5th or b3; bars open on a chord tone.

## 2. Density and space
- Two textures: a "crammed" wall of notes and slower melodic lines; dense bursts even over a slow blues, the contrast part of the effect [S].
- [I] Melodic phrases 4-8 notes a bar; bursts 12-24 (8th triplets up to sextuplets); 1-2 beats' rest between phrases; 8-16 bars in songs.
- He planned only the first phrase and a "link phrase", then improvised [S]. [I] Arc: a melodic opening statement, development, rising register and denser repeated licks, a peak.

## 3. Phrasing
- Thematic repetition across his solos; short licks repeated to build tension [S]; descending three-note motifs in sequence [S].
- [I] A 3-6 note cell repeated 3-8 times, then broken out of with a held bend; phrases 1-2 bars, sometimes across the bar line; a short lick answered by a held bend.
- "Spontaneous", "sloppy", part of the appeal [S]. [I] Timing +/-15-25 ms, velocity spread +/-15.

## 4. Rhythm
- Triplet pentatonic sequences, three notes a beat, "commonplace" [S]; fast pull-off triplets moved through the boxes [S]; descending 16th runs [S]; syncopated runs [S].
- [I] Sextuplet bursts (unconfirmed); bursts slightly ahead (+5-10 ms), held bends on or just behind the beat.

## 5. Bends
- Large bends: 1.5 and 2 tones, over-bends [S]; unison bends on G and B at key moments [S]; pre-bends that "cry" on release [S]; double stops [S].
- [I] Whole step about 60%, half 20%, 1.5-2 steps 15%, quarter tones on b3 5%; fast (50-120 ms to pitch); 1-2 bends a bar in melodic sections.

## 6. Vibrato
- Fast, even, "shimmering", the vibrato on a bent note a signature [S]; also "wide, controlled" [S] (the sources differ on width).
- [I] 6-7 Hz, +/-0.3-0.5 semitone, wider on bent notes.

## 7. Other techniques
- Pull-off runs and hammer-ons [S]; pull-offs to open strings [S] ([I] open E/B/G inside runs in E or A); an open string bent behind the nut while picked repeatedly [S] ([I] one pitch repeated with a pitch-bend wobble); slides and double stops [S].
- The 12th-position box, moved to other boxes in the key [S]; [I] start mid-neck, end high (15th-20th fret).
- Bow and theremin: out of scope.

## 8. Out of scope for MIDI
Tone (Les Paul/Telecaster, Marshalls, gain, room), echo/backwards echo, wah, studio layering, bow, theremin, pick attack, string noise; over-bends and pitch drift only roughly (the bend range limits them).

## 9. Recipe
1. Minor pentatonic over the key's root; the b5 and 2 now and then; the natural 6 if the harmony is Dorian.
2. A written-feeling opening: two bars, 4-6 notes a bar, a chord tone on the downbeat, one whole-step bend with fast vibrato, a rest.
3. A "link" phrase: the opening cell again in another box, varied.
4. Tension by repetition: a 3-6 note triplet pull-off cell looped 4-8 times, climbing through boxes or staying put.
5. Release with a big bend: a 1.5-2 step over-bend or a unison bend, held with wide vibrato.
6. A burst: a bar of crammed sextuplets or descending 16ths ending on the root (open-string pull-offs in E or A).
7. End high: a pre-bend released to a chord tone, or a held bend over the last chord.
8. Loose humanizing: +/-20 ms, bursts slightly ahead, b3s a quarter tone sharp, uneven velocities.
