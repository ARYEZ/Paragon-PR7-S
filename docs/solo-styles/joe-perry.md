# Joe Perry: lead style (technique sheet, revised from the full articles)

Researched for the studio's solo engine. Techniques and habits only; no solo or melody of his is written out here.
**[S]** = an article supports it (named), **[I]** = inference. No source measures anything: numbers are [I] unless marked. Lick Library pages are sales copy for paid lessons (weaker evidence).

## What changed from the first sheet
- Swing: 2:1 (about 67%), not 58-62% (MusicRadar/Total Guitar: "downstrokes twice as long as upstrokes"); Guitar World says "many" songs swing, so swing is a per-song switch.
- The major 6th added to minor pentatonic, alone and in double stops; minor and major 3rd both (Guitar World).
- Behind the beat now sourced (Richard Fortus, Guitar World: "He places notes behind the beat... always bending and weaving around the beat... microtonal notes"). The first sheet's "lazy, behind-the-beat bends" quote appears in no article (a search-summary paraphrase); "precise bends" was the learner's precision, not Perry's.
- Space, call and response, the solo's arc and varied vibrato speed now sourced; new techniques sourced: pinch harmonics, whammy, tapped harmonics, alternate-picked bursts, legato runs, open strings, sliding 6ths.
- The "schooled" contrast is Whitford's own quote (Guitar Player 2019).

## 1. Note choice
- Minor pentatonic and blues as the base [S, almost every lesson]; major pentatonic and Mixolydian mixed in [S, MusicRadar; Lick Library "Don't Get Mad, Get Even"].
- The major 6th added, alone and in double stops; minor and major 3rd together, bends up to the major 3rd [S, Guitar World, MusicRadar].
- Subtle chromatic passing notes [S, Lick Library]; [I] about 10% of notes.
- [I] Pool: about 60% minor pentatonic + b5, 25% the 6, major 3rd and 2, 15% chromatic/other.
- "Strictly feel", no theory [S, Guitar Player 2005]: [I] loose targeting, the root, b7 or 5 at phrase ends only.

## 2. Density and space
- Space "to the max" [S, Guitar World]; "not... a million notes per second" [S, Lick Library]; "doesn't sound like he's in a hurry" [S, Guitar World].
- Arc [S, Lick Library, several lessons]: opens with bends and slides, "midway" shifts into faster alternate-picked and legato licks.
- [I] 4-8 notes a bar sparse, bursts of 12-16 for half a bar to a bar; rests 1-4 beats; 8-16 bars.

## 3. Phrasing
- An opening line "answered" by another [S, Guitar World]; "fluid and sharp", following the vocal [S, Lick Library]; rarely the same twice [S, MusicRadar]: vary a motif's repeats.
- [I] Phrases 1-2 bars, motifs repeated 2-4 times, off-beat or pickup-bend starts, long vibrato'd endings.

## 4. Rhythm
- "Highly rhythmic"; swung 16ths and off-beat rhythms; 8th-note shuffles [S, Guitar World]; swing 2:1 [S, MusicRadar]; rhythm "what I form everything around", Chuck Berry "a four-year college course in rhythm" [S, Premier Guitar].
- Behind the beat [S, Fortus]; loose, "never too polished" [S, Guitar Player 2005]; "tight yet loose" [S, Guitar World].
- [I] Swing 0.65-0.67 when on; a third of starts off the 8th grid; 10-25 ms late (bends most); jitter +/-10-15 ms, velocity +/-15.

## 5. Bends
- Constant bending, microtones round the line [S, Fortus]; bends and slides open his solos, quick bends too [S, Lick Library]; full and half steps [S]; a 1.5-step bend as an answer [S, Guitar World]; double-stop bends [S]; unison bends [S]; .008 strings so even the low strings bend [S, Premier Guitar].
- [I] 1-3 bends every 2 bars: whole 60%, half 30%, 1.5 steps 10%; often bend and release; up over 1/8-1/4 beat; about 20% double-stop or unison near the climax; slight drift (+/-10-20 cents) on some held notes.

## 6. Vibrato
- Wide and vocal [S, Lick Library]; "rich and varied... slow, like a cry, or a faster one" [S, Lick Library].
- [I] +/-0.5-1 semitone, 4-7 Hz chosen per note, from about 30% into the note, on notes of half a beat or more and bend tops.

## 7. Other techniques
- Pull-offs, slides and bends, "slippery" [S, Guitar World]; legato runs [S]; open strings in leads [S, Fortus].
- Double stops, aggressive ones with the 6th [S, Guitar World]; sliding 6ths [S, MusicRadar].
- Pinch harmonics, whammy, tapped harmonics [S, Lick Library].
- Slide in open G, A, E and DADGAD; some lap steel [S, Premier Guitar, MusicRadar, Guitar World].
- [I] One or two pentatonic boxes, a box higher for the climax.

## 8. Out of scope for MIDI
Tone, guitars, Plexi amps, light fuzz and boost, pick or no pick, feedback, the talk box, real slide (pitch bend only approximates it), pick noise; pinch harmonics only through a sampler's articulation.

## 9. Recipe
1. Pool: minor pentatonic + b5, the major 6th and major 3rd; Mixolydian over dominant chords; a rare chromatic passing note.
2. Open with 1-2 sparse bars of bends and slides; build with repeated, varied motifs; fast legato or alternate-picked bursts midway; end on a held vibrato note.
3. 1-2 bar phrases in call and response, 1-4 beats' rest between.
4. Swing 0.66 if the song swings; a third of starts syncopated; everything 10-25 ms late.
5. Bend constantly: whole and half steps, an occasional 1.5; double-stop and unison bends near the climax.
6. Slides into notes, pull-off groups, 6th double stops, an occasional open string, a pinch harmonic as a peak.
7. Varied vibrato on long notes and bend tops.
8. Loose humanizing: never too polished.
