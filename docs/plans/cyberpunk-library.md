# Plan: the CYBERPUNK world learned from the owner's library (agreed 2026-11-24, waiting for files)

The owner has cyberpunk folders scanned into the MediaBay (MIDI and WAV). They agreed to the plan below;
it waits for a file listing of one or two of those folders (names only) and two or three of the MIDI files
(read for shapes only, never committed).

## Order (the owner: "that's the way to build it")

1. **The files as options.** Each MIDI file in the chosen folders becomes an option in the keys lane's
   CYBERPUNK world beside our 13 parts (`cyberpunkKeysOptions`): its notes refitted to the section's chords
   and key (each note's place in the chord kept, as Follow Chord Track's `followPitch` / the library drums),
   the player's rhythm, voicing, register and feel kept. In the option browser with pictures, HEAR, favourites.
2. **Habits learned from them** (as LEARN A DRUMMER): sixteenth weights, density per section, voicing spread,
   arpeggio direction, hold lengths, colours (sus2, add9, the flat six); our generated parts follow them.
3. **From the WAVs:** rhythms (gates, stabs, pumping shapes) and clean single-note lines turned into notes
   (VariAudio's pitch reading). Not pads or chords (polyphonic transcription isn't reliable).

## Choices proposed (confirm with the owner)

- Folders: those with "cyberpunk" (or synthwave / darksynth) in their path, plus a HUB SETTINGS page to tick
  folders on and off (as GROOVE FOLDERS).
- Lanes: keys first; then files sorted by name words (0.225.0's lists) and note range into bass, lead and
  drums within the cyberpunk world.
